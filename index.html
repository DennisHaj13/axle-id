1 Photograph and measure

Lay the axle flat in good light. Put the reference on the axle, shoot square-on from the middle, and keep the whole axle in frame. A longer reference gives a tighter number on the long measurements.

Open the printable reference card (print at 100%).

2 Check the numbers

Type over anything the photo got wrong. Fractions work: 89 1/4, 2-3/8 or 2.375. Confirm with a tape before ordering.
